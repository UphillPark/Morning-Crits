Front page Education · Schools of architecture · Cuts · England

A school of architecture without its name: Birmingham City University’s head of architecture leaves after 15 months, in a department folded into a larger school and stripped of two master’s courses

The Architects’ Journal (5 November) reports that Peter Baldwin, appointed head of architecture at Birmingham City University in July 2025, left at the end of October. The university declined to say why. Paul Laycock, previously deputy head, is interim head, while the staff union disputes a further round of cuts that may reach the department again.

According to the AJ, architecture was moved from its own department into the School of Architecture, Built Environment, Computing and Engineering, and lost the historic name of the Birmingham School of Architecture, which dates from 1908. This summer’s restructuring ended several master’s-level courses linked to architecture and the built environment, including Design Management and Landscape Architecture. The university told the AJ the courses were closed because insufficient student numbers made them financially unviable, and that the old name “remains an important part of the University’s heritage”.

The local branch of the University and College Union calls the closures short-sighted and says a further round puts 72 full-time education posts at risk, with some losses likely in the school. It also objects to plans to hire new staff through a subsidiary company, which it says would create a two-tier workforce. For an applicant the story is a practical one: a course title tells you little about who will teach it in two years’ time. The AJ also notes that many architecture staff chose negotiation over public protest, and that negotiations ultimately failed. Everything here comes from a single outlet (the AJ), so no figure appears in the headline.

Scotland · Liability · Cladding (3 Nov)

Twelve years after completion, an architect is still in court over cladding — and has pulled the insulation maker in with it

The Architects’ Journal (3 November) reports that BDP and the contractor Galliford Try failed to have a claim over alleged cladding defects at the Leonardo Inn Hotel in Union Square, Aberdeen thrown out as time-barred. The hotel was completed in 2009, and Leonardo began proceedings in 2021. In a ruling dated 20 October, Lord Richardson rejected several of Leonardo’s arguments but let it pursue the case that the firms’ conduct kept it unaware of the alleged defects. Those allegations remain to be tested at trial.

BDP has also brought in Kingspan as a third party, alleging misrepresentation about its Kooltherm K15 insulation, a step the judgment says followed the Grenfell Tower Inquiry reports. The judge accepted that a misrepresentation can be a continuing act, so the time limit did not necessarily start at installation. The AJ says the firms and Kingspan had been approached for comment. Single source (the AJ).

England · Profession · Labour (3 Nov)

A union writes to practices before the law does: architectural workers’ branch reminds employers of a new duty to explain the right to join

The Architects’ Journal (3 November) reports that the Section for Architectural Workers (SAW), a branch of Unite the Union, has mailed practices employing more than 10 people about the Employment Rights Act 2025. From January, employers must give new staff a written statement of their right to join a union, and existing staff must be reminded annually by 5 April next year or told where to find it. The government has said it will supply a standard statement that employers may amend only to add workplace information.

SAW describes itself as the only trade union specifically for architectural workers in the UK. Its member Alfie Gee says many in the profession “either don’t know what a union is or increasingly are scared that their boss will discriminate against them if they join”. Other changes due next year include cutting the qualifying period for unfair dismissal claims from two years to six months. Single source (the AJ).

London · Housing · Small sites (5 Nov)

A three-bedroom house in a Croydon back garden gets consent in two months — its architect credits a rule change, not a better argument

The Architects’ Journal (5 November) reports that Working Title Architects has won planning permission for a 92m² self-build house at 189 Kingsdown Avenue, Croydon, after applying in August and securing consent in October despite “distinctly negative” pre-application advice from the council. Founder James Bazeley says submitting on the day the biodiversity net gain regulations changed (6 August) meant the small scheme avoided the slow process of agreeing contributions with the council.

The plot of 775m² is divided so that the new home and its garden take about 300m², leaving the existing house roughly 200m² of rear garden. Officers had first seen “little prospect of approval” because the scheme was “out of keeping” with the suburb, so the practice redrew the roofs and materials after Arts and Crafts neighbours: red multi-stock brick, roughcast, lime render and clay tiles, with an all-electric servicing strategy. Single source (the AJ).

국민평형과 소형의 간격이 좁아진다: 대출 한도가 집의 크기를 다시 정한다

연합뉴스(11월 9일)는 부동산 리서치업체 리얼투데이가 국토교통부 실거래가를 분석한 결과를 전했다. 올해 9월 서울 아파트 전용 84㎡ 평균가는 13억110만 원, 59㎡는 11억3,216만 원으로 차이가 1억6,894만 원이었다. 1년 전 같은 기간의 2억4,026만 원보다 29.7% 줄었다. 강남3구는 4억2,445만 원에서 2억9,636만 원으로, 노도강은 1억4,055만 원에서 1억302만 원으로 줄었다. 기사는 원인으로 지난해 10월 15일 대책의 대출 한도 축소(시가 15억 원 이하 6억 원, 15억 초과 25억 이하 4억 원, 25억 초과 2억 원)를 지목하는 리서치업체 연구원의 분석을 인용한다. 같은 지면의 사진 설명에는 지난달 주택담보대출 금리가 4.88%로 14년 5개월 만의 최고라고 적혀 있다. 민간 리서치 분석이고 단일 출처라 제목에는 수치를 쓰지 않았다.

소방서의 비용은 어느 정부가 내는가: 당정이 지방 소방 재정에 중앙 지원을 검토한다

연합뉴스(11월 8일)에 따르면 더불어민주당과 정부, 청와대는 8일 고위당정협의회에서 소방의 긴급한 필수 투자를 위해 중앙정부가 지방정부에 2조 원 수준의 재정을 지원하는 방안과 추가 확충을 검토하기로 했다. 현재 소방 예산의 국비 비중은 10% 안팎이고, 2020년 소방공무원이 국가직으로 전환됐지만 상당 부분을 지방자치단체가 부담한다. 당정은 화재진압 장비, 훈련 시설, 소방 특화 의료체계 예산을 예산안 심의에 반영하기로 했으며, 재원이 미래대응기금인지 일반회계인지는 추후 결정하기로 했다. 논의는 경기도 부천 공장 화재에 투입된 소방위의 순직 이후 본격화됐다고 기사는 적는다. 국민의힘은 이 발표를 ‘물타기용’이라고 비판했다(연합뉴스 제목 기준, 본문 미확인, 2등급).

남산에 작가정원 다섯 곳: 박람회가 끝난 뒤에도 남는 정원은 누가 돌보는가
