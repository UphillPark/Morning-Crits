Front page Housing · Public value · Awards

The 2026 Stirling Prize goes to 157 homes on a car park — Croydon’s council-built Lion Green Road is building of the year

At the 30th-anniversary ceremony at Old Billingsgate on the night of 15 October, Lion Green Road by Mary Duggan Architects and RUFFARCHITECTS took the prize. Started by a council development company and finished by the council after that company was wound down, the scheme raises the question of whether the criteria for building of the year have shifted from form towards housing and ownership.

According to the Architects’ Journal (15 October), the winner is a 157-home scheme on a sloping council-owned car park in Coulsdon, in the south London borough of Croydon. Five tridecagonal blocks of up to seven storeys are linked by landscaped paths and shared courtyards. ArchDaily (15 October) reports that the five blocks share one construction specification but differ in height, orientation and brick bond, giving each its own identity. The roughly 8m fall across the site was not levelled; the buildings step with the topography, in three tones of brown brick chosen to sit with the surrounding green. The scheme is described as home to 635 residents. It was begun by Brick By Brick, Croydon Council’s development company, and completed by the council after the company was wound down (AJ).

Ownership is the other half of this award. According to the AJ, the homes are split evenly between market and affordable tenures, and the council holds 85 affordable rented homes on a long lease, sub-let to a housing association. The AJ reports that the whole project was sold to Regen Capital for £38 million. Jury chair and RIBA president Chris Williamson said density can be achieved without compromising the quality of place; the citation called it “an innovative and imaginative approach to high-density suburban housing”. Mary Duggan said architectural ambition, social value and generosity can coexist within today’s development realities. The scheme was a 6/1 outsider with William Hill and came third in the AJ readers’ poll. The editor’s reading: this is a year in which “who owns it” stayed in the jury’s story.

Discourse · Biennale · Europe

UK · Reinvention · Retail

An empty department store becomes offices and a nursery — Oru Sutton wins the RIBA Reinvention Award

According to the Architects’ Journal (15 October), the RIBA 2026 Reinvention Award went to Oru Sutton, the conversion of a former British Home Stores (BHS) shop on Sutton High Street in south London. Designed by Samuel Chisholm Studio and Takero Shimazaki Architects, the 3,400㎡ building holds co-working space, a nursery, a café and a fitness studio. The existing structure of the vacant store was altered as little as possible, and a community roof garden was added with a pond, trees, planters, rainwater storage and chickens.

Jury chair Hugh Broughton said it had turned a “dark, unloved and forgotten space” into a bright, lively destination. He praised the generosity of the spaces, the quality of daylight, the modular construction and a “less is more” approach to the existing structure, and said it shows how empty high streets can be revived. It reads well against the closed Seoul hypermarket in the 6 October issue.

CRITHow do you draw, in section, the act of bringing light into the deep plan of an old department store? Between “minimal intervention” and “new use”, where does the architect’s work stop?

UK · Conservation · Stephen Lawrence

Redruth’s Buttermarket — a Grade II market restored for £3.4m wins both the Stephen Lawrence Prize and the first Conservation Award

According to the AJ (15 October), the Buttermarket in Redruth, Cornwall, is a heritage-led regeneration of a Grade II listed building and market yard within a UNESCO World Heritage Site. It was designed by Architectural Thread (project architect Jen Boddington), cost £3.4 million and was commissioned by Redruth Revival, a local community interest company. It won the RIBA Stephen Lawrence Prize 2026 and the newly created Conservation Award.

Stephen Lawrence Prize jury chair Matthew Goldschmied called it a story of trust and interdependence shaped by architectural skill, and praised the passion and creativity of Boddington, for whom a project of this type was a first. Conservation Award jury chair Liz Smith singled out the careful work of stripping away unsympathetic additions, saying it celebrates Cornwall’s industrial heritage through “a balance of conservation and innovative design”. Read it alongside Donald Insall’s “living buildings” on yesterday’s front page.

CRITWhen a market yard is reopened, how does the drawing tell what was revived from what was removed? When the client is a community organisation, what spatial form does trust take?

Germany · Timber hybrid · Federal

A federal building on Berlin’s Spreebogen — timber modules, concrete cores, and a temporary presidential office built for its next tenant

According to ArchDaily’s weekly review of 15 October, sauerbruch hutton has completed an office building for federal institutions on the Spreebogen in Berlin. The two buildings, 18,700㎡ in total, are a timber hybrid: prefabricated timber office modules combined with reinforced-concrete cores and a plinth. Since July 2026 they have served as the temporary office of Federal President Frank-Walter Steinmeier while Schloss Bellevue is refurbished.

The façade sets coloured balustrades over fluted glazed ceramics, so that the building reads differently from each direction. It is presented as designed around circular construction and future adaptability, so that another federal body can move in later. Timber volumes and carbon figures were not given in the weekly review and could not be checked. It is a building for “the next occupant”, recalling the Seoul ministry buildings emptied by the move to Sejong (board, 14 October).

CRITWhat structural grid should a building have when it does not know its next occupant? Of the timber modules and the concrete core, which holds the present and which the future?

Czechia · Social housing · Urban block

Prague, Kodaňská 1441 — social housing at the corner of a block whose courtyard has become a permeable ground

According to ArchDaily (15 October), the Portuguese practice OODA placed municipal social housing at the corner of a Prague block, with commercial units along the perimeter and ground-floor homes facing the courtyard. The courtyard links a square, gardens, play space, seating and footpaths; the public passages open by day and close at night.

The article reports a 21.5% increase in permeable surface in the courtyard, with water collection, infiltration gardens and bio-filter roofs, and plans to increase permeable surface further in a second phase. The floor area and completion date could not be clearly confirmed from the article. Together with the shared courtyards of today’s front page, it asks: whose is an open courtyard at night?

CRITHow does a passage that opens only by day change the relationship between social-housing residents and their neighbours? With what lines and colours would you show a 21.5% gain in permeable surface on plan?

  • Copper strips the infrastructure. According to the AJ (15 October), the green copper bands on the Culling Road ventilation shaft in Southwark, London, have been almost entirely torn off. 둘로 나뉘는 LH: ‘짓는 조직’과 ‘맡는 조직’의 경계는 한 단지의 어디에 그어지는가

(10월 12일 국감 기사로 72시간을 넘긴 배경 이슈다.) MTN에 따르면 12일 국회 국토교통위 LH 국정감사에서 LH 부채는 지난해 173조 6,567억 원이었고, LH가 국토위에 낸 자료상 2030년 372조 8,000억 원으로 늘어날 전망이다(기사 제목은 373조, 본문은 372조 8,000억 원). 정부는 LH를 택지개발·주택건설을 맡는 주택도시개발공사와 임대·주거복지를 맡는 주택도시자산공사로 나누려 한다. 민주당 의원은 임대사업 손실이 2021년 1조 7,900억 원에서 지난해 2조 7,300억 원으로 늘었다며 부채 배분 방안을 물었다. 이성훈 사장은 공사 원가가 최근 몇 년간 50% 가까이 올랐다며 정부에 재정 지원을 요청 중이고, 기존 부채·적자의 배분안은 아직 없다고 답했다. 국민의힘 의원들은 올해 공공주택 착공이 목표에 크게 못 미친다고, 서류상 착공 신고만으로 실적을 인정하는 방식을 비판했다. 사장은 대부분 물량이 조달청 사업자 선정 단계이며 11~12월 중 착공이 많다고 설명했다.

한 동의 단면에 ‘지을 때 쓴 돈’과 ‘고칠 때 드는 돈’을 부위별로 겹친 비용 단면, 두 조직의 담당 구역 경계를 단지 배치도 위에 그린 도면. 비용 값은 가상으로 둔다.

지하로 몰린 물: Istanbul 폭우에서 주지사는 지하층 주민에게 윗층으로 올라가라고 했다

Al Jazeera(10월 15일)에 따르면 Istanbul 폭우로 14세 소년이 Fatih의 Balat에서 물웅덩이에 들어갔다가 감전돼 숨졌다. 주지사실은 구조 시도에도 숨졌다며 조사에 들어갔고, Al Jazeera가 확인한 CCTV 영상이 이를 보여준다. 가장 많은 비는 아시아 쪽 Beykoz의 Omerli에서 약 194mm였고, 직무대행 시장 Nuri Aslan은 일부 지역에 12시간 동안 100mm 가까이 내렸다고 했다. 당국은 침수 1,277건과 산사태 15건을 집계했고 47명이 고립됐으며, 800채 넘는 주택과 사업장 280곳이 피해를 입었다. 지하차도와 도로가 잠기고, 병원 두 곳에 물이 들어오고, Haciosman 지하철역 천장으로 물이 샜으며, Sultangazi의 초등학교 옹벽이 학생들이 있는 중에 무너졌다(다친 학생은 없었다). 주지사 Davut Gul은 목요일 꼭 필요하지 않으면 집에 머물라고 했고 지하층 주택 주민은 높은 층으로 옮기라고 했다. 도시 전역 휴교와 오토바이 통행 금지가 내려졌다. 이번 폭우를 설명하는 기상 당국의 입장은 기사에 나오지 않아 확인하지 못했다.
